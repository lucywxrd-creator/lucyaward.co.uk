About

I'm Lucy Ward, a music journalist, PR and marketer based in London. I write new-music reviews, live and festival reviews and features, and I've covered festivals including Field Day, Love Supreme, Wilderness, LIDO and All Points East with press accreditation.

From December 2024 to December 2025 I completed a Level 4 Music PR and Communications apprenticeship at Different Sauce, a London music PR and influencer marketing agency, working across press, partnerships, social, release admin and new-business outreach.

Now I work freelance for artists, festivals, charities and brands, and I'm currently on retainer for a health and wellness brand.
